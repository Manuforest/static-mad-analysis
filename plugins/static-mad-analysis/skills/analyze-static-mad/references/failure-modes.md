# Failure-mode audit

Use this compact audit before finalizing. Detailed procedures live in the core and conditional references.

## Evidence and reconstruction

- **Language-prior bypass**: infer plot from title, synopsis, or familiarity instead of the video.
- **Uniform-sampling blindness**: miss inserts, action order, text, reactions, or match cuts.
- **Identity drift**: merge or rename entities after crop, age, costume, style, or polarity changes.
- **Chronology collapse**: treat adjacency or playback order as shared physical time and space.
- **Contact-sheet overreach**: infer motion or action phase from widely spaced thumbnails.
- **VLM continuity hallucination**: smooth cuts, crop changes, and layer animation into physical action.
- **Single-story lock-in**: settle on one fluent explanation before testing alternatives.
- **Evidence laundering**: present source knowledge, reception, or creator notes as blind observation.

## Subject, event, and arrangement

- **Role collapse**: equate visual, shot, action, focal, dramatic, thematic, or discourse subject.
- **Event-only summary**: list incidents without responses, state changes, or consequences.
- **Summary-as-story**: mistake broad source-event coverage for narrative progression.
- **Subject-drift blindness**: miss an unsupported move among character, relationship, group, and abstract thesis.
- **Shot-handoff blindness**: praise individual shots without checking focal, gaze, direction, scale, motion, shape, or tonal transfer.
- **Fixed-symbol dictionary**: assign one permanent meaning to a changing color, object, or effect.
- **Template overfit**: impose a standard song or story structure that the work does not use.

## Media provenance and context

- **Adapter-by-label**: select rules from the upload tag rather than observed behavior.
- **Motion provenance blindness**: confuse depicted action, source footage, retiming, crop/layer motion, and transition motion.
- **Source/edit causality collapse**: attribute an edit-created relation to the source scene or vice versa.
- **Speech ownership collapse**: assume the visible person speaks, hears, endorses, or is addressed by a quotation.
- **Original-context omission**: avoid source facts needed to identify entities, scenes, chronology, or editorial transformation.
- **Original-work comparison creep**: turn context into fidelity, completeness, omitted-information, accessibility, canon, or competition judgment.
- **Author-intent absolutism**: treat creator explanation as the only valid observable effect.

## Craft, music, and reception

- **Motion worship**: reward constant movement and ignore meaningful holds.
- **Craft-proxy bias**: use software, 3D, layer count, rendering cost, visible effort, or effect count as quality.
- **Surface-style imitation**: confuse copied gradients, typography, shadows, or transitions with an authored system.
- **Music reduction**: equate beat synchronization with phrase, section, emotional, or semantic alignment.
- **Rhetoric without operation**: use “狂气,” “高级,” “爽,” “意识流,” or “意境” without observable mechanisms.
- **Reception-as-truth**: use comments, reactions, popularity, or reviewer authority to replace video evidence.
- **Comment-era collapse**: mix initial reception with later virality, adaptation news, nostalgia, or fandom discussion.
- **Competition-judgment creep**: convert diagnostic language into ranking, award, eligibility, or contest-fitness claims.

## Final rule

Keep editing recognition, interpretation, original-work context, creator intent, craft judgment, and reception evidence distinct. Explain mechanisms and effects rather than reducing the work to one scalar.
