# Static-MAD adapter

Use when still images, manga panels, sprites, text, masks, layers, and compositing carry most depicted change.

## Central ambiguity

Separate **what the source image depicts** from **what the edit makes happen**. A pan across a still face may change attention without depicting head movement; two characters composited into one frame may create a relational proposition without proving a shared physical scene.

## Motion classes

Label meaningful change as:

- `depicted_action`: action already represented inside the image;
- `camera_or_crop_motion`: reframing, search, approach, withdrawal, or reveal;
- `layer_or_mask_motion`: parallax, separation, occlusion, disclosure, fracture, or transformation;
- `typography_motion`: linguistic or graphic action;
- `transition_motion`: change primarily joining shots or states;
- `uncertain_constructed_motion`.

Do not convert these classes into literal character action without adjacent evidence.

## Material logic

Record visible operations as selection/crop, separation/redraw, compositing/reconstruction, fabricated composite, external asset, treatment-only, or uncertain. Compare the base composition with what animation adds: hierarchy, spatial relation, kinetic form, atmosphere, spectacle, knowledge, emotional distance, or rhythm.

Track repeated source images across changed crop, color, context, text, and music. Treat them as possible memory, correction, accumulation, obsession, refrain, bookend, or economy—not automatically as new events.

## Static-specific reading

Read adjacency as syntax and examine:

- negative space and implied off-screen partners;
- body fragments and relocated agency;
- screen-within-screen observation and control;
- typography as narrative, thought, lyric echo, label, interface, diagnosis, or texture;
- graphic matches and fabricated spaces;
- visual-change, processing, semantic, and reading density as distinct quantities;
- holds as active duration rather than lack of technique.

Use [community-reading-framework.md](community-reading-framework.md) for community vocabulary and [montage-grammar.md](montage-grammar.md) for detailed operations.

## Return to the core

Return material observations to the core when they change subject, event, time-space, viewer knowledge, or editorial thesis. Within the same analysis, also retain graphic design, virtual camera, motion design, 3D construction, compositing, transition precision, atmosphere, or spectacle when those are the operation's actual value; do not invent a narrative function.
