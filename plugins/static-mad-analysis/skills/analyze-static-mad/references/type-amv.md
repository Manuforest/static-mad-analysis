# AMV adapter — provisional extension

Status: **provisional**. Use only when the user explicitly requests AMV analysis or asks to test the shared core beyond static MAD. This adapter is an exploratory vocabulary, not a complete AMV theory or a settled analysis standard. Label its report section `provisional_extension` and preserve competing descriptions when the vocabulary does not fit cleanly.

Use when continuous source footage supplies substantial character, camera, or environmental motion.

## Central ambiguity

Separate **source-scene causality** from **edit-created relation**. A reaction shot may originally answer one event but be reassigned by the AMV to another character, place, time, lyric, or idea.

## Continuity layers

For important cuts, record three layers:

1. `within_clip_continuity` — what visibly unfolds inside the selected footage;
2. `source_scene_relation` — only when source context is available and requested;
3. `edit_created_relation` — what selection, trimming, juxtaposition, repetition, speed, sound, or music makes the shots mean here.

Do not use familiarity with the source to fill the second layer during the blind pass.

## AMV-specific checks

- Track whether action match, eyeline, screen direction, and camera movement continue physically or only graphically.
- Identify cross-scene subject consistency: what lets one emotional or thematic line persist across costume, age, location, episode, or character changes?
- Test whether a visible reaction belongs to the immediately preceding edited event or is recontextualized.
- Examine omitted beginnings and endings of clips: trimming can change agency, cause, outcome, and emotional valence.
- Track speed changes, reversals, freeze frames, loops, optical flow, and retiming before treating motion as source-natural.
- Distinguish scene-level emotion from the larger emotional direction created by recurrence and music.

## Return to the core

Use these distinctions to update subject roles, event-response chains, chronology, and editorial thesis. The target is not to reconstruct the source plot but to explain the new audiovisual proposition made by this edit.
