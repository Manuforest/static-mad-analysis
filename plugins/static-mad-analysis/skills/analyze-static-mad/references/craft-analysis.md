# Edited-video craft analysis

Use this only when the user requests critique, comparison, technique analysis, or improvement advice. Inspect craft after the blind pass, but do not require every technique to carry narrative meaning. Formal pleasure, perceptual control, atmosphere, kinetic energy, visual identity, and technical display are valid functions in themselves.

## Explanatory pattern

For every important observation, use:

`timestamped evidence -> operation -> execution qualities -> formal/perceptual/narrative/display function(s) -> limitation or plausible alternative`

Do not force a narrative reading or assign each passage to a separate craft channel. Describe whichever functions the same operation actually supports: it may change meaning, control attention, sustain atmosphere, organize rhythm, enrich texture, bridge structure, display technical control, or simply produce visual pleasure. Several may coexist, and decorative value is not automatically dismissive. Use the lenses below as open questions within one integrated reading, not pass/fail checks.

## Analysis lenses

| Lens | Questions |
|---|---|
| Editorial coherence | What relationship, contradiction, state change, or idea organizes the work? How does it develop? |
| Narrative organization | Are events followed by reactions and state changes? How are causal gaps, repetition, and convergence used? |
| Emotional construction | How do duration, expression, juxtaposition, sound, and recurrence accumulate or redirect emotion? |
| Subject/focalization | How do visual subject, action subject, focalizer, and dramatic subject align or diverge? |
| Discourse organization | When speech leads, how do speaker, visible subject, assigned narrator, addressee, and editorial position align or diverge? |
| Source/edit relation | For footage, which continuities and reactions belong to the selected scene, and which relations are newly created by the edit? |
| Material preparation | How do crop, separation, redraw, restoration, edge, resolution, and texture affect the image? |
| Graphic design | How do grid, alignment, hierarchy, balance, scale, shape language, negative space, typography, color, value, texture, and repetition work as a designed surface? |
| Single-frame composition | How do mass, direction, depth, figure-ground separation, cropping, text, and motif placement guide attention or create visual tension? |
| Motion design and virtual camera | How do framing, lens impression, pan, track, orbit, roll, zoom, parallax, easing, acceleration, motion paths, focus, and holds create spatial or kinetic experience? |
| 3D and spatial construction | Do perspective, camera geometry, depth layers, occlusion, scale, lighting, shadows, reflections, materials, particles, depth of field, and motion blur form a credible or deliberately graphic space? |
| Compositing and integration | How consistently do matte edges, light direction, color response, grain, sharpness, blur, atmosphere, contact shadows, and interaction bind heterogeneous elements? |
| Transition design | Are cuts, wipes, morphs, match moves, camera passes, object transitions, and spatial handoffs precise, surprising, rhythmically controlled, or intentionally conspicuous? |
| Visual systems | How do color, borders, line treatment, typography, interfaces, and recurring images change function? |
| Music/edit architecture | How do beat, phrase, section, energy, lyric, hold, release, and counterpoint organize the edit? |
| Motion and reading time | Does motion amplitude fit the passage? Do text and expressions receive enough time? What does stillness emphasize? |
| Visual identity | Which repeated decisions make the work internally recognizable rather than merely busy? |
| Atmosphere | How do image, space, sound, pace, motif, and absence sustain a psychological state? |
| Spectacle and virtuosity | What is pleasurable because it is precise, dense, risky, novel, excessive, spatially convincing, or visibly controlled—even when it adds no plot information? Does the work stage a reveal or escalation around its own technique? |
| Technical finish | Do jitter, halos, clipping, banding, text legibility, flash load, encoding, or accidental discontinuity change the experience? |

## Execution questions

Within the same passage-level analysis, assess visible execution without reducing quality to narrative usefulness:

- **control**: intentional timing, repeatable motion logic, stable alignment, deliberate edges, and managed density;
- **coherence**: graphic, spatial, lighting, texture, and motion decisions belong to a readable system;
- **precision**: matches, masks, camera paths, typography, rhythm, and contact relationships land cleanly;
- **spatial credibility**: perspective, parallax, occlusion, scale, focus, and light agree—or break consistently for a graphic aim;
- **integration**: 2D, 3D, live action, interface, particles, and text feel intentionally related rather than accidentally stacked;
- **variation and escalation**: a technique develops, recombines, contrasts, or culminates instead of repeating at one intensity;
- **legibility under density**: spectacle preserves the intended focal path, or deliberately overwhelms it with controlled consequences;
- **distinctiveness**: the treatment produces a recognizable solution rather than only reproducing a familiar preset language.

Recognize deliberate spectacle. A technically conspicuous passage may function as a climax, signature, change of register, rhythmic payoff, or pure audiovisual display. It need not be justified as character psychology.

Infer only what the output supports. Do not name software, plugins, node setups, layer counts, production time, or difficulty as fact without project files, breakdowns, or creator documentation. Distinguish visible complexity from verified labor or technical difficulty.

## Reporting craft

- Lead with mechanisms and effects, not adjectives.
- Translate words such as “爽快,” “压抑,” “高级,” or “有意境” into observable duration, contrast, density, motion, sound, and composition.
- Distinguish visual-change density, processing density, semantic density, and reading demand.
- Name the operative mechanism: source action, constructed motion, retiming, juxtaposition, sound bridge, or recontextualized speech.
- Report strong formal or technical passages even when they do not affect story interpretation.
- Explain a limitation only when evidence shows what control, coherence, precision, spatial credibility, integration, rhythm, clarity, comfort, or intended excess it disrupts.
- When suggesting a revision, preserve the work's apparent aim and identify the smallest change that would clarify or strengthen it.
