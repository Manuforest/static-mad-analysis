# Edited-video craft analysis

Use when the user requests critique, comparison, technique analysis, or improvement advice. Let the shared core own subject, event, time-space, and interpretation. Let [community-reading-framework.md](community-reading-framework.md) own arrangement and reception diagnostics.

Do not require every technique to carry narrative meaning. Formal pleasure, perceptual control, atmosphere, kinetic energy, visual identity, and technical display are valid functions.

## Explanatory pattern

For each important passage, use:

`visible or audible result -> operation -> relation to adjacent or recurring elements -> viewing effect -> event, relationship, information, or structural change -> limitation or plausible implementation when useful`

Describe whichever functions are supported. One operation may control attention, organize rhythm, sustain atmosphere, change meaning, enrich texture, bridge structure, display control, or produce visual pleasure. Do not split these into independent scoring channels.

Do not end a discussion at “mask reveal,” “graphic match,” “parallax,” “color change,” or another technique name. When narrative material is present, explain what the operation connects, withholds, reassigns, recalls, or prepares. If its value is primarily formal, spatial, decorative, atmospheric, or spectacular, say so directly without inventing a story function.

## Craft lenses

| Lens | Questions |
|---|---|
| Material preparation | How do crop, separation, redraw, restoration, edge, resolution, and texture affect the visible result? |
| Graphic design | How do grid, alignment, hierarchy, balance, scale, shape, negative space, typography, color, value, texture, and repetition organize the surface? |
| Single-frame composition | How do mass, direction, depth, figure-ground separation, cropping, text, and motif placement guide attention or create tension? |
| Motion design and virtual camera | How do framing, lens impression, pan, track, orbit, roll, zoom, parallax, easing, acceleration, paths, focus, and holds create spatial or kinetic experience? |
| 3D and spatial construction | Do perspective, geometry, depth layers, occlusion, scale, light, shadow, reflection, materials, particles, depth of field, and motion blur form a credible or deliberately graphic space? |
| Compositing and integration | Do matte edges, light direction, color response, grain, sharpness, blur, atmosphere, contact shadows, and interaction bind heterogeneous elements? |
| Transition and shot handoff | Do cuts, wipes, morphs, match moves, camera passes, gaze, focal position, screen direction, motion vectors, scale, shapes, or color masses connect or deliberately rupture adjacent shots? |
| Visual systems | How do color, borders, line treatment, typography, interfaces, and recurring images change function across the work? |
| Music/edit architecture | How do cuts, movement, phrases, sections, energy, semantics, holds, release, and counterpoint organize the passage? Use the four synchronization levels in the community framework when detailed diagnosis is needed. |
| Motion and reading time | Does motion amplitude fit the passage? Do text and expressions receive enough time? What does stillness emphasize? |
| Material economy and disclosure | Does crop, withholding, expansion, recurrence, or recontextualization change what the viewer can know? Are privileged images allocated in a way that supports the work's actual progression? |
| Text and image | Who speaks or writes, what changes through the line, can it be read and absorbed, and do image and music supplement, answer, contradict, pause for, or compete with it? |
| Atmosphere | How do image, space, sound, pace, motif, and absence sustain a psychological state? |
| Spectacle and virtuosity | What is pleasurable because it is precise, dense, risky, novel, excessive, spatially convincing, or visibly controlled? How is the display revealed or escalated? |
| Technical finish | Do jitter, halos, clipping, banding, text legibility, flash load, encoding, or accidental discontinuity change the experience? |

## Execution qualities

- **control**: timing, motion logic, alignment, edges, and density feel intentional;
- **coherence**: graphic, spatial, lighting, texture, and motion decisions form a readable system;
- **precision**: matches, masks, paths, typography, rhythm, and contact relationships land cleanly;
- **spatial credibility**: perspective, parallax, occlusion, scale, focus, and light agree—or break consistently for a graphic aim;
- **integration**: 2D, 3D, live action, interfaces, particles, and text feel intentionally related;
- **variation and escalation**: a technique develops, contrasts, recombines, or culminates rather than repeating at one intensity;
- **legibility under density**: spectacle preserves the intended focal path or overwhelms it with controlled consequences;
- **recovery after intensity**: a flash, transition, dense layout, or rapid text entrance is followed by enough stability for the intended image or line to register;
- **distinctiveness**: treatment produces a recognizable solution rather than only reproducing preset language.

Recognize deliberate spectacle. A conspicuous passage may serve as climax, signature, register change, rhythmic payoff, or pure audiovisual display.

Infer only what the output supports. Do not name software, plugins, node setups, layer counts, production time, or difficulty without project files, breakdowns, or creator documentation. Distinguish visible complexity from verified labor.

## Reporting

- Lead with mechanisms and effects rather than adjectives.
- Translate “爽快,” “压抑,” “高级,” and “有意境” into the cuts, holds, movement, sound, composition, contrast, and reading experience that produce the response.
- Use density distinctions to think internally. In the response, describe what changes quickly, what demands attention, and what receives time to settle.
- Replace mechanism tags with concrete language: the source character moves, the crop travels across a still, two separate images are placed together, or one sound continues across a cut.
- Report strong formal or technical passages even when they do not affect story interpretation.
- Explain limitations through disrupted control, coherence, precision, spatial credibility, integration, rhythm, clarity, comfort, or intended excess.
- Give revision advice only when requested; preserve the apparent aim and propose the smallest useful change.
- Do not cite detector output, audio measurements, sampling, confidence values, or workflow completion as craft evidence. Use them privately to locate passages, then verify the visible or audible result.
- Prefer natural uncertainty such as “the character appears to have been separated from the panel” over public evidence labels such as `possible_implementation`.
- Follow repeated techniques across the work. Explain how a later use changes because the event, character relation, musical position, or viewer knowledge has changed.
- Judge text volume and source-asset volume only through their consequences. Diagnose reading conflict, emotional stasis, premature disclosure, exhausted reuse, or missed payoff instead of praising scarcity or criticizing abundance.
- Organize a technical response around the work's actual passages and developing problems. Do not force separate sections for composition, color, motion, music, and narrative.
