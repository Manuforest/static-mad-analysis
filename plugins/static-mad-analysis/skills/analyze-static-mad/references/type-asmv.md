# ASMV adapter — provisional extension

Status: **provisional**. Use only when the user explicitly requests ASMV analysis or asks to test dialogue-led extensions of the shared core. The discourse-role vocabulary is exploratory and may not cover every ASMV structure. Label its report section `provisional_extension` and do not force every passage into the listed roles.

Use when dialogue, monologue, narration, quotation, or voice bridges organize the video's argument or emotional development. Combine this with the static adapter, or the provisional AMV adapter when explicitly requested, according to the image material.

## Central ambiguity

Separate the people involved in a line of speech:

- `original_speaker`: who visibly or audibly says it in the source clip;
- `original_addressee`: whom the source scene addresses, only when supported;
- `visible_subject`: who is shown while the line plays;
- `assigned_narrator`: whose perspective the edit makes the line seem to voice;
- `discourse_subject`: whose idea, judgment, memory, or appeal structures the passage;
- `implied_addressee`: whom the edited sequence appears to address;
- `editorial_position`: the broader stance formed by selection and arrangement.

These roles may coincide, diverge, or remain indeterminate.

## Speech operations

Record whether a line functions as dialogue, testimony, memory, thesis, counterclaim, refrain, command, confession, accusation, irony, thematic quotation, or sound texture. Support the classification through placement, visible speaker cues, answer structure, recurrence, music, and later consequences.

Inspect:

- voice bridges across characters, scenes, and times;
- cutaways that accept, contradict, reinterpret, or ironize the line;
- dialogue splicing, omission, reordered clauses, and changed addressees;
- music ducking, silence, overlap, reverberation, filtering, and abrupt interruption;
- pronoun reference and whether the visible person is falsely recruited as speaker;
- how multiple quotations accumulate into one discourse without sharing a literal conversation.

Transcribe only what is needed for analysis and mark uncertain words. Do not treat lyrics as dialogue unless the edit explicitly assigns them.

## Argument-response chains

Alongside event-response chains, use:

`proposition -> audiovisual response -> reframing or state change -> later consequence`

The response may be another line, an image, silence, music entry, contradiction, or delayed recurrence.

## Return to the core

Use discourse roles and proposition chains to revise focalization, viewer-disclosure time, event causality, thematic subject, and editorial thesis. Do not reduce the video to a transcript summary.
