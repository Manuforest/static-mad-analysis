## Material construction and treatment

Record crop, separation/redraw, compositing/reconstruction, fabricated composites, treatment-only passages, and external assets where relevant. Avoid claims about the original arrangement unless comparison was explicitly requested.

## Montage and global expression systems

Explain how adjacency, repetition, typography, color, borders, line treatment, processing density, and compositing change meaning across the work.

## Music, motion, and reading time

Analyze phrase structure, accumulation/release, motion amplitude, easing, holds, text legibility, and dynamic/static contrast.

## Alternatives and unresolved questions

Preserve ambiguity, counterevidence, and the most useful intervals to revisit.

## Optional external-context revision

Only when requested, report how external materials confirm, correct, weaken, or merely inspire the blind reading.

## Craft analysis

When requested, explain craft as timestamped evidence, operation, effect, and limitation or alternative. Give revision priorities only when the user asks for advice.
